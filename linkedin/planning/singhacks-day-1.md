# SingHacks 2026 — Day 1 LinkedIn Post

Often in hackathons, the people invent problems rather than solutions. 

They  invent a problem, add an AI agent, add a payment screen, and work backwards until the story sounds convincing.

Day 1 at SingHacks 2026 was a useful reset.

The Ripple brief was blunt: start with a real customer problem. Then build the shortest credible path from need → discovery → decision → payment → value delivered.

That sounds obvious. It is harder than it looks.

The opening sessions made the point from different angles.

Cyrus Cruz from Tenity shared how a team of students built a strong prototype in two and a half days. They didnt ship an application, but  they had a real enough problem to focus on and iterated on it.

Shang Cheong from Julius Bär framed the wealth-management challenge as moving from portfolio monitoring to intelligence: what does a client’s portfolio look like, what should the relationship manager know, and what should happen next — without removing the human relationship from the process. 

On the Ripple track, the room was asked to look past the transaction itself. One slide described the current state of agentic payments as “lots of specs, SDKs, and hype.”

The target is the part that matters: real-world use cases.

The Oliver Wyman session supplied the counterweight. An agent can be capable and still need boundaries.

Who authorised it? What was it allowed to do? What did it actually do? Can someone reconstruct the decision later?

The throughline that my team - The Fast and the Fungible ( ) - had picked up was that in this agentic age , the value is not to replace the human completely , it is to make the human the orchestrator and auditor while the agents did most of the grunt work. 

Day 2 is the harder part: proving the loop.

Can an agent discover something useful, make a bounded decision, pay on XRPL, and deliver an outcome that someone actually wants?

I’m not sharing the product yet. But it starts with a problem that exists whether or not blockchain does.

Small. Useful. Real.
